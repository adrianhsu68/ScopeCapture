ScopeCapture Oscilloscope Capture — Windows 10/11 x64

Getting started
Copy ScopeCapture.exe to your PC and double-click it. No installer, Python,
VISA runtime, or Keysight IO Libraries are required on the target computer.

1. Power on the oscilloscope and connect it to a network reachable from the PC.
2. Open ScopeCapture.exe. Choose English or 繁體中文 at the top right.
3. Check the subnet and click Find scopes. Click the correct model and serial
   number in the list. Use the serial number to distinguish identical models.
   Alternatively, enter the scope's IPv4 address and click Identify & select.
4. Choose a screenshot folder and a black or white background.
5. Click Capture screenshot. A color PNG is saved with a timestamp, model, and
   serial number in its filename. The image opens automatically unless disabled.

Preferences
The app remembers your language, last selected instrument and IP, subnet,
save folder, background, and automatic image opening preference.
Preferences are per Windows user, stored at:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ScopeCapture\settings.json
If an older version already saved a folder, that exact path is kept when you
switch languages. New installations default to Pictures\ScopeCaptures.
On first use the app chooses Traditional Chinese for a Chinese system locale,
otherwise English. Existing preferences without a language retain Chinese.

Supported models and verification
Keysight EDUX1052A/G, DSOX1202A/G, and DSOX1204A/G.
DSOX1204A: discovery, capture, black/white backgrounds, and restoration tested
on hardware. EDUX1052A: confirmed working on hardware by a user. The remaining models use
the same documented command family, but still await hardware verification. Model support does not imply
that every model or firmware version has been independently verified.
Other SCPI instruments may appear in search results but cannot be captured.
Connection: IPv4, raw SCPI TCP port 5025. USB connections are not implemented.

Background colors
Black (default) follows the screen colors; white is convenient for printing.
The app temporarily changes Ink Saver if needed, then restores and verifies
the original value. It does not change acquisition settings, Run/Stop, or Autoscale.
If a transfer times out or is incomplete, no further commands are sent. A warning
explains that Ink Saver may not have been restored; check the instrument panel.
To capture a particular event, stop the scope using its front panel first.

Troubleshooting
- Check the scope's IP address in Utility / I/O / LAN on the front panel.
- Suggested subnets use /24; change this if your network uses another range.
  Search accepts at most a /22 subnet (1024 addresses).
- Search covers only the entered subnet. For another reachable subnet, enter
  its range or use Connect by IP. Firewalls or VLAN isolation may block TCP 5025.
- A remembered instrument is not proof it is online. Its model and serial are
  checked on every capture. If DHCP changes its IP, search again and select it.
- App messages follow the selected language. Native Windows file dialogs follow
  your Windows language settings. English system errors include a numeric code.
- This executable is unsigned. If company policy blocks it, ask IT to review it;
  do not disable security software.
